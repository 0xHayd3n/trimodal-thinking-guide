# Contributing

Corrections should identify the exact **TechRxiv v1** section and one-based PDF page, explain the issue, and distinguish the author's proposal from external background and companion analysis. Preserve all author credit and the source's date discrepancy.

Do not add invented results, unsupported bias/safety guarantees, guessed clipped table wording, or a later-version change without explicitly documenting its source and scope. New examples must be labeled original and hypothetical unless independently supported.

Update affected modules, source/claim metadata, and citations together. Keep source PDFs, full paper text, copied original figures, and private Library transfer data out of this repository. Run `python scripts/validate_repository.py`; verify changed external destinations separately where access allows.
